# SAMOSBOR — VISUAL TARGET 01

Status: APPROVED
Version: 0.1

## Purpose

Главный визуальный якорь production-пакета персонажей SAMOSBOR. Все следующие player/NPC/enemy concepts должны сохранять этот уровень материальности, силуэтов, износа и изометрической читаемости.

## Style lock

- Soviet industrial doomwave;
- late-Soviet / post-Soviet utilitarian materiality;
- worn but maintained clothing and equipment;
- canvas, leather, painted steel, rubber, old plastics, bakelite;
- muted industrial palette;
- repair marks, inventory markings and serial production feel;
- realistic game concept art, not cartoon and not photorealistic portrait photography;
- Diablo/Path-of-Exile-like 3/4 top-down gameplay readability;
- practical silhouettes over decorative microdetail.

## Character language

Humans must look like people who live inside a functioning but exhausted megastructure. Clothing is layered, repaired, re-used and modular. Avoid generic tactical operators, cyberpunk streetwear and costume-like post-apocalypse designs.

## Enemy language

Enemies remain materially connected to the same world. Altered humans are deformations of recognizable workers/residents rather than fantasy monsters. Industrial enemies look like service/repair machinery whose function has become hostile or corrupted.

## Production rule

No asset may silently redefine the project style. When a new concept conflicts with this contract, the approved visual target wins until the user explicitly approves a new direction.
